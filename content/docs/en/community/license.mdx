## Using and distributing Everywhere

You can run the AGPL version at home or at work, including for commercial purposes. When you redistribute a copy, keep the required notices and license. When you distribute a modified version, identify your changes and license the covered work under the AGPL. If you distribute a binary, provide its Corresponding Source by one of the methods in section 6 of the [license](https://github.com/Sylinko/Everywhere/blob/main/LICENSE).

If you modify Everywhere and users interact with your modified version remotely over a network, section 13 requires that version to prominently offer those users access to its Corresponding Source, free of charge.

For proprietary distribution of the host application or other custom licensing terms, contact [Sylinko Inc.](mailto:contact@sylinko.com). As the [commercial licensing notice](https://github.com/Sylinko/Everywhere/blob/main/LICENSE-COMMERCIAL.md) explains, the commercial license takes effect when both parties sign a separate agreement. A subscription alone covers only the official hosted services described in the [Terms of Service](/en/policies/terms).

## Developing an independent Mod

Under the [Mod Interface Additional Permission](https://github.com/Sylinko/Everywhere/blob/main/EXCEPTIONS), you can compile, link, load and distribute a qualifying independent Mod under a license of your choice, including a proprietary license. To qualify:

- Use only the documented Mod loading mechanism and public host or Mod service interfaces. The Mod must be independent and must not modify, fork or redistribute the host application.
- Do not alter, hide, spoof, intercept, replace, disable, bypass or impair account and authentication, Cloud Sync, the official model gateway, Mod verification or repository trust controls. The same rule applies to official links, attribution, copyright and license notices, and sponsorship or donation channels.

The host application itself remains subject to its license. If you distribute your own AGPL fork, you may extend this additional permission to your version or remove the exception statement. Choose a distinct name, identifier and artwork as described in the [brand policy](/en/policies/trademark).

The open-source license for first-party Mods is AGPL-3.0-only. Designated SDK, contract, versioning and packaging libraries use Apache-2.0 as stated in their directory licenses. Third-party components carry their own licenses, listed in the [third-party notices](https://github.com/Sylinko/Everywhere/blob/main/ThirdPartyNotices.txt).
