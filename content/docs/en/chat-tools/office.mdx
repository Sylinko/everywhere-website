**Office Suite** includes a built-in Office document engine across **Windows, macOS, and Linux**, letting your assistant work directly with `.docx`, `.xlsx`, and `.pptx` documents with no local Office installation required.

<Callout type="tip">
The built-in **Read File** tool does not parse `.docx`, `.xlsx`, or `.pptx` files—when working with Office documents, Everywhere uses **Office Suite** instead.
</Callout>
