## 🤔 What is Everywhere? [#what-is-everywhere]

**Everywhere** is an interactive AI assistant with on-screen awareness, featuring a modern UI and integrated desktop tools across Windows, macOS, and Linux.

Unlike traditional AI tools, Everywhere understands what is currently on your screen. Instead of taking screenshots, copying text, and switching windows, just press a shortcut or highlight text to get instant help right where you work.

## ✨ Key Capabilities [#key-capabilities]

- 👁️ **Screen Awareness**: Combines vision capabilities with native accessibility and UI automation across Windows, macOS, and Linux to read structured screen context with minimal disruption.
- 🤖 **Flexible Model Choices**: Start immediately with **Everywhere Cloud** (Native Mode), pick from 14 **Built-in Providers** (Preset Mode), or connect any **Custom Protocol** endpoint (Advanced Mode, including Ollama and self-hosted models). See <DynamicLink href="/[lang]/docs/models">Models & Providers</DynamicLink>.
- 📝 **Prompts & Quick Configuration**: Build reusable system prompts with **Quick configuration** preset templates, dynamic placeholders, and a **Live preview**. See <DynamicLink href="/[lang]/docs/prompts">Prompts</DynamicLink>.
- 🧩 **Skills & Tools**: Extend your assistants with reusable skill files, built-in tools (**File Operations**, **Terminal**, **Web**, **Office Suite**, **Screen Awareness**, and **Essential** tools), and **MCP Server** integrations—protected by three **Tool approval** modes (**Request approval**, **Approve for me**, and **Full access**). See <DynamicLink href="/[lang]/docs/skills">Skills</DynamicLink> and <DynamicLink href="/[lang]/docs/chat-tools">Tools</DynamicLink>.
- ✨ **Selection Toolbar**: Highlight text in any app to pop up the floating **Selection Toolbar** to translate, explain, summarize, rewrite, or click **Free input** on the spot. See <DynamicLink href="/[lang]/docs/text-selection">Text Selection</DynamicLink>.
- ⚡ **Smooth Chat Workflow**: Queue follow-up messages or steer the current reply immediately while the assistant is responding, compact long threads automatically or manually, and configure preferences across **Settings** (**Common**, **Display**, **Shortcuts**, **Proxy**, **Chat Window**, **System Assistant**, and **Text selection**).
