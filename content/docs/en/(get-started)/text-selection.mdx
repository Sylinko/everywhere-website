When you highlight text in another app, Everywhere can pop up a compact **Selection Toolbar** right next to your selection so you can run quick AI actions or ask follow-up questions without switching windows.

## Built-In & Custom Actions

Everywhere includes 6 built-in selection actions:

- **Translate**: Translates selected text into your preferred/system language while preserving meaning and tone.
- **Explain / Define**: Explains words, concepts, names, or code snippets concisely.
- **Summarize**: Summarizes longer passages (appears automatically when you select 50 or more characters).
- **Rewrite / Improve**: Fixes grammar/spelling and improves clarity and flow.
- **Research**: Searches the web to verify facts and gather background context.
- **Check Grammar**: Lists grammar, spelling, and punctuation issues along with corrected text.

In **Settings > Text selection > Manage Actions**, you can:
- Turn individual actions on or off and **drag to reorder** them.
- Customize any built-in action's **Name**, icon, label visibility, and **Prompt** (with one-click reset), or click **Add Custom Action**.
- Set **Maximum number of tools** shown directly on the toolbar (`0–12`, default `5`); extra enabled actions move into the **More** menu.

## Free Input

When **Allow free-form input** is enabled (on by default), click **Free input** on the toolbar to open an inline input box right next to your selection and ask anything about the highlighted text.

### Selection toolbar

- **Enable Selection Toolbar**: Master toggle for the floating selection toolbar.
- **Toolbar Preview**: Interactive preview of the current toolbar layout.
- **Toolbar opacity** (`0.1–1.0`, default `1.0`) & **Adjust opacity based on pointer distance**: Gently fades the toolbar when your cursor moves away and restores it as you move back.
- **Allow free-form input**: Shows or hides the free-form question input button on the toolbar.
- **Maximum number of tools** & **Manage Actions**: Configure action order, prompts, and how many buttons appear directly on the bar.

### Chat window

- **Automatically add to chat**: Automatically attaches selected text and its app context to the chat window when you highlight text (works independently of the floating toolbar).

### Advanced

- **Disable in fullscreen applications** *(default: on)*: Stops monitoring selections while a fullscreen app (such as a game or fullscreen video) is active.
- **Use temporary chats from the toolbar** *(default: on)*: Starts toolbar chats as temporary chats that clear automatically when Everywhere exits. Turn this off to keep toolbar chats in regular history.
- **Applications with text selection disabled**: Completely disables text selection capture in listed apps (pick an open window, browse for an app, or enter a process name / macOS bundle ID).
- **Applications with the selection toolbar hidden**: Hides the floating toolbar in listed apps while still allowing automatic attachment to chat. You can also choose **Don't show in this application** directly from the toolbar's **More** menu.

<Callout type="info" title="Platform Notes">
- **Windows**: Turn on **Settings > Common > Install as a service** if you need text selection inside elevated (administrator) windows.
- **macOS**: Requires **Accessibility** permission in **System Settings > Privacy & Security**; app exclusion rules match macOS bundle identifiers.
- **Linux**: Selection toolbar availability depends on your desktop environment (supported on Hyprland; not yet supported on X11, KDE Plasma, GNOME, or Niri).
</Callout>
