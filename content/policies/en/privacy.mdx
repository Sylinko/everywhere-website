## 1. What information do we collect?

We collect information that you provide to us, as well as information automatically collected when you use our Services.

### Information You Provide & We Collect Automatically

**Personal & Account Information**
- **Account Details:** Name, email address, contact data, billing address, and other profile information you voluntarily provide.
- **Social Media Login Data:** If you register using social media accounts (like Google or GitHub), we collect basic profile information (name, email and profile picture) from the provider.
- **Payment Data:** In standard checkout, including Alipay, Sylinko is the merchant and Stripe processes payments on our behalf. In eligible regions, if checkout says “Sold through Link” (Stripe Managed Payments), Stripe or its affiliate is the merchant of record and an independent controller for the transaction, invoicing and tax. Using the Link wallet alone does not change the merchant. Stripe collects identifying information about the devices that connect to its services. Stripe uses this information to operate and improve the services it provides to us, including for fraud detection. Stripe also acts as a controller for its own fraud prevention and legal obligations. You can learn more about Stripe and read its privacy policy at https://stripe.com/privacy.
- **Sensitive Information:** We do not ask for sensitive information to create an account.

**Everywhere Application Data (Desktop)**
*Crucially, our desktop application is designed with a "Local First" privacy architecture.*
- **Desktop Context & Screen Data:** To provide context-aware assistance, Everywhere may process content displayed on your screen (via screenshots or accessibility APIs). **This processing is performed locally on your device.** We do not upload raw screen recordings or full screenshots to our servers unless you explicitly trigger a feature that requires cloud processing (e.g., sending a specific screenshot to an LLM).
- **Chat History & Settings:** Stored locally on your device by default. If you enable cloud sync, encrypted data is stored on our servers to facilitate synchronization.
- **Telemetry & Diagnostics:** We use **Sentry** to monitor performance and stability. To protect your privacy:
    - **PII Scrubbing:** The desktop app omits email, username and IP address from Sentry user fields; Passport filters sensitive data from error reports and logs.
    - **Pseudonymous Identifiers:** The desktop app uses a random identifier stored on your device. Passport uses a one-way hash of your account ID.
    - **Diagnostic Controls:** The desktop essential-data setting can still send errors, metrics and higher-severity logs. Optional Passport session replay is subject to the [consent controls below](#5-do-we-use-cookies-and-other-tracking-technologies).
- **Push Notifications:** We may request to send notifications regarding your account or features. You can opt out in your device settings.

**Service Usage & Device Data**
- **Log & Usage Data:** IP address, browser/device characteristics, operating system, language preferences, and usage timestamps.
- **Device Data:** Device IDs, hardware model, and system configuration.
- **Location Data:** Approximate location (country/city) derived from IP address. We do not collect precise GPS data.

**Data Controller:** For the purposes of the GDPR and UK GDPR, Sylinko Inc. is the Data Controller of your personal information.

## 3. What legal bases do we rely on to process your personal information?

We process your personal information only when we have a valid legal reason to do so. Depending on your location and how you interact with our Services, we rely on the following legal bases:

- **Consent:** When you have given us permission to use your information for a specific purpose. You can withdraw your consent at any time.
- **Performance of a Contract:** To fulfill our contractual obligations to you (e.g., providing the Services you requested).
- **Legitimate Interests:** For our legitimate business interests, such as improving our Services, security, and fraud prevention, provided these interests do not override your fundamental rights and freedoms.
- **Legal Obligations:** To comply with applicable laws, regulations, or legal requests (e.g., tax laws, law enforcement requests).
- **Vital Interests:** To protect your vital interests or those of others (e.g., in emergencies).

*For specific rights related to your region (such as GDPR for EU/UK or PIPEDA for Canada), please refer to the "[What are your privacy rights?](#13-what-are-your-privacy-rights)" section below.*

## 5. Do we use cookies and other tracking technologies?

We use cookies and similar tracking technologies (such as SDKs and local storage) to provide, secure, and improve our Services. By integrating these details here, we aim to be transparent about how we track data.

**Types of Technologies We Use:**

- **Strictly Necessary:** Essential for the Services to function, such as Sylinko Passport's authentication tokens. These cannot be disabled.
- **Functional & Preferences:** Used to remember your settings (e.g., theme preferences, language) and provide enhanced features.
- **Analytics:** Google Analytics and masked Passport session replay are off by default. They require your explicit analytics consent through Cloudflare Zaraz. Replay masks text and inputs and blocks media.

**How to Control Cookies:**
- **Browser Settings:** You can set your browser to refuse all or some browser cookies, or to alert you when websites set or access cookies.
- **Do Not Track:** Note that our systems may not currently respond to "Do Not Track" (DNT) signals from browsers, but we honor your explicit opt-out choices in our app settings. The website and Passport also recognize Global Privacy Control (GPC). With GPC on and no prior explicit site choice, optional tracking stays off without a prompt. Passport never starts replay while GPC is on.

- **[Cookie preferences](/en/policies/cookie):** Change or withdraw consent in **Cookie Preferences** in the website footer or **Cookie Settings** in the Passport footer. Withdrawal stops recording and clears the replay session marker.

## 6. Do we offer artificial intelligence-based products?

Yes, our Services include AI-powered features. We prioritize your privacy when integrating these technologies.

**Use of AI Technologies**  
We utilize third-party AI Service Providers (such as Anthropic, DeepSeek, and others listed in settings) to power our AI features. Your inputs and context are shared with these providers solely to generate responses.

**API Relay Services & Data Privacy**
When you use our API relay services, your prompts and the AI's responses are transmitted through our servers to third-party Model Providers.
- **No Training by Sylinko:** We do not use your prompts, screen context, or outputs to train our own models. However, third-party providers' use of your data is governed by their own policies (e.g., [OpenAI Privacy Policy](https://openai.com/privacy), [Anthropic Privacy Policy](https://www.anthropic.com/privacy)).
- **No Permanent Content Storage:** We do not permanently store the content of your prompts or completions.
- **Metadata Only:** We retain only metadata (such as token usage, timestamps, and model type) for billing and abuse prevention purposes.

## 7. Documentation Feedback & GitHub Discussions

Our documentation website includes a feedback feature that allows you to rate pages and submit comments. When you use this feature:

**How Feedback Works:**
- Your feedback (including your rating and any text comments) is transmitted through our GitHub App to **GitHub Discussions**, a public forum hosted by GitHub, Inc. (a Microsoft company).
- By submitting feedback, you acknowledge and consent that your feedback content will be publicly visible on our GitHub repository's Discussions page.

**Data Processing & Third-Party Disclosure:**
- **Public Visibility:** All feedback submitted through this mechanism becomes publicly accessible on GitHub. Do not include personal or sensitive information in your feedback.
- **Third-Party Processing:** GitHub's collection and processing of your data is governed by [GitHub's Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- **Data Storage Location:** GitHub's servers are located primarily in the United States.
- **No Account Required:** You may submit feedback without creating a Sylinko account. However, your feedback will be associated with the page URL and timestamp.

**Your Rights:**
- Under GDPR, CCPA, and PIPL, you have the right to request deletion of your feedback. However, due to the public nature of GitHub Discussions and the way the platform works, removal requests must be coordinated with both Sylinko and GitHub.
- To request removal of feedback you have submitted, please contact us at [contact@sylinko.com](mailto:contact@sylinko.com) with the relevant GitHub Discussion URL.

**Cross-Border Data Transfer (GDPR/PIPL Compliance):**
- When you submit feedback, your data is transferred to GitHub's servers in the United States.
- For the safeguards that apply to international transfers, see [section 9](#9-is-your-information-transferred-internationally).
- Where applicable law requires separate consent for this transfer, we must obtain it before transferring your data.

## 10. How long do we keep your information?

We will only keep your personal information for as long as it is necessary for the purposes set out in this Privacy Notice, unless a longer retention period is required or permitted by law (such as tax, accounting, or other legal requirements). Under the updated Passport deletion process, we immediately delete the active profile, sessions and verification records and initiate deletion of the avatar and CloudSync database. We remove identifying details from retained billing records and keep necessary financial and subscription records for up to seven years for tax and accounting. Stripe may retain historical invoices under its own legal obligations. On the updated service, later authentication activity clears expired sessions and verification records for active accounts.

When we have no ongoing legitimate business need to process your personal information, we will either delete or anonymize such information, or, if this is not possible (for example, because your personal information has been stored in backup archives), then we will securely store your personal information and isolate it from any further processing until deletion is possible.

## 13. What are your privacy rights?

We believe you should have control over your information, regardless of where you live. We generally apply a high standard of privacy to all users. Depending on your location (e.g., EEA, UK, Canada, California, etc.), you may have specific rights under applicable laws.

**Your Rights Include:**
- **Right to Access:** You can request a copy of the personal information we hold about you.
- **Right to Rectification:** You can request that we correct inaccurate or incomplete information.
- **Right to Erasure (Deletion):** You can request that we delete your personal information, subject to certain legal exceptions (e.g., tax records).
- **Right to Restrict Processing:** You can ask us to limit how we use your data.
- **Right to Data Portability:** You can request a copy of your data in a structured, machine-readable format.
- **Right to Opt-Out:** You can opt out of marketing communications and, where applicable, the sale or sharing of personal data for targeted advertising.
- **Right to Withdraw Consent:** If we rely on consent to process your data, you can withdraw it at any time.

**How to Exercise Your Rights**
To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at [contact@sylinko.com](mailto:contact@sylinko.com). We will consider and act upon any request in accordance with applicable data protection laws.

**Additional Regional Information:**
- **EEA/UK/Switzerland:** You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
- **US States (e.g., California):** We do not sell your personal information or share it for cross-context behavioral advertising. We respect Global Privacy Control (GPC) signals where applicable.
