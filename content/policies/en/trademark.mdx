Sylinko Inc. reserves the Sylinko brand, the identity of its official Everywhere releases and its original brand artwork. The project's [software licenses](/en/docs/community/license) exclude those assets. The policy addresses confusion about official origin and makes no general claim to the ordinary English word “everywhere”. The full policy and the list of reserved artwork are in [TRADEMARK.md](https://github.com/Sylinko/Everywhere/blob/main/TRADEMARK.md).

## Describing compatibility

You may use the names Everywhere and Sylinko in accurate factual references. For example, “MyPlugin for Everywhere” can describe a compatible Mod. You may link to the official website or repository and keep required copyright and attribution notices when redistributing source.

## Naming and publishing third-party work

- **Mods, packages and unofficial repositories.** Choose your own identifier prefix. The prefixes `everywhere.*` and `sylinko.*` are reserved unless Sylinko Inc. gives prior written permission.
- **Modified builds and forks.** To distribute a modified build, use a distinct application name and identifier and replace the reserved artwork, including the listed app icons and banner. Reusing those assets or confusingly similar derivatives in a modified build, fork or third-party product requires prior written permission.
- **Official status.** Names, icons, and descriptions of third-party builds, services or Mods must not imply official origin, sponsorship, certification or endorsement by Sylinko without prior written permission.

Sponsor artwork and third-party promotional materials belong to their respective owners. For permission to use Sylinko branding, contact [Sylinko Inc.](mailto:contact@sylinko.com).
