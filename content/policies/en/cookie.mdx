## 1. Introduction

This Cookie Policy explains how **Sylinko Inc.** ("we," "us," and "our") uses cookies and similar technologies (such as local storage and web beacons) when you visit our websites, including our **Documentation Site** and **Sylinko Passport** (collectively, the "Websites").

This policy provides you with clear information about the cookies we use and your choices regarding these technologies.

## 2. What are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently, as well as to provide information to the owners of the site.

We use two main types of cookies:

* **Session Cookies:** These are temporary cookies that expire when you close your browser.
* **Persistent Cookies:** These remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them.

We also use **"Similar Technologies"** such as **Local Storage** (used to store your theme preferences, like "Dark Mode") which function similarly to cookies but store data in your browser for longer periods.

## 3. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

1. **Authentication & Security:** To identify you when you log in to Sylinko Passport and to protect our services from malicious attacks (e.g., bot attacks).
2. **Functionality:** To remember your preferences, such as your language settings or theme (Light/Dark mode).
3. **Performance & Analytics:** To understand how our Websites are used, detect errors, and improve system stability.

## 4. Cookies We Use

Below is a detailed list of the cookies and technologies we use on our Websites:

### 4.1. Strictly Necessary Cookies (Essential)

These cookies are essential for the Websites to function properly. You cannot switch these off in our systems.

| Cookie Name                               | Provider         | Purpose                                                                                                                     |
| ----------------------------------------- | ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `__Secure-sylinko-passport.session_token` | Sylinko Passport | **Authentication.** Keeps you signed in (up to 7 days per renewal).                                                         |
| `__Secure-sylinko-passport.session_data`  | Sylinko Passport | **Authentication.** Caches encrypted session data for up to 30 seconds.                                                     |
| `__Secure-sylinko-passport.state`         | Sylinko Passport | **Authentication.** Helps prevent CSRF during third-party sign-in; expires after 5 minutes.                                 |
| `cf_clearance` / `_cfuvid`                | Cloudflare       | **Security.** Records a passed challenge or, where enabled, distinguishes visitors sharing an IP address for rate limiting. |
| `cf_consent`                              | Cloudflare       | **Consent Management.** Used by Cloudflare Zaraz to manage your cookie consent preferences.                                 |

### 4.2. Functional Cookies (Preferences)

- **Language.** The Everywhere website uses the language in the page URL. If the URL does not specify a language, the site uses your browser's language preference, defaulting to English. Sylinko Passport remembers your language choice in the `NEXT_LOCALE` cookie for up to one year.
- **Appearance.** The `theme` setting in local storage remembers your choice of light or dark mode.
- **Passport preferences.** `sidebar_state` remembers whether the sidebar is open for 7 days. `better-auth.last_used_login_method` remembers your last sign-in method for 30 days.

### 4.3. Performance & Analytics Cookies

These cookies allow us to count visits and traffic sources so we can measure and improve the performance of our site.

| Name                        | Provider         | Purpose                                                                                                                      |
| --------------------------- | ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `sentryReplaySession`       | Sentry           | **Session replay.** Links a masked Passport replay to the current tab (session storage, not a cookie).                       |
| `_ga`, `_ga_<container-id>` | Google Analytics | **Analytics.** Distinguish browsers and visits on the Everywhere website.                                                    |
| `cfz_google-analytics_v4`   | Cloudflare Zaraz | **Analytics.** Stores analytics identifiers for the website’s Google Analytics integration.                                  |
| `cfzs_google-analytics_v4`  | Cloudflare Zaraz | **Analytics.** Stores session information for the same integration.                                                          |

Passport does not write `sentryReplaySession` by default. Replay requires analytics consent through Zaraz and stays off while GPC is on. It masks text and form inputs, blocks media and excludes sensitive pages such as password resets. Withdrawing consent stops recording and removes the entry.

## 5. Third-Party Services

In addition to our own cookies, we use trusted third-party services that may verify cookies on your device. Specifically:

* **Stripe:** We use Stripe for payment processing. Stripe sets cookies to detect fraud and facilitate transactions. (See [Stripe Cookie Policy](https://stripe.com/legal/cookies-policy))
* **Cloudflare:** (See [Cloudflare Privacy Policy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/))
  * We use Cloudflare Turnstile for security. A Cloudflare challenge may set a cookie once you pass it.
  * We also use Cloudflare Web Analytics for anonymous website traffic analysis.
* **Sentry:** We use Sentry for error tracking and performance monitoring, with personal identifiers removed or masked. (See [Sentry Privacy Policy](https://sentry.io/privacy/))
* **Google Analytics:** We use Google Analytics for website traffic analysis. (See [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy))

## 6. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You have the right to decide whether to accept or reject cookies. You can exercise your cookie rights by setting your preferences in the Cookie Consent Manager or through your browser settings. Here are some ways to manage your cookie preferences:

* **Cloudflare Zaraz:** We use Cloudflare Zaraz to manage cookie consent. When you first visit our site, analytics are off by default. Choose **Accept All**, **Reject All** or **Preferences**. To change or withdraw consent, open **Cookie Preferences** in the website footer or **Cookie Settings** in the Passport footer.
* **Global Privacy Control (GPC):** If your browser sends GPC and you have no saved choice, both sites automatically reject optional tracking without showing a prompt. You can change a saved choice in the footer. Passport never records replays while GPC is on.
* **Browser Settings:** Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings preferences. You can usually find these settings in the "Options" or "Preferences" menu of your browser.
* **Consequences of Disabling:** Please note that if you disable **Strictly Necessary Cookies** (like authentication tokens), you may not be able to use certain features of our Websites, such as logging in to Sylinko Passport.
